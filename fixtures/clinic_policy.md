# Fictional clinic policy — teaching data

Version: demo-v1. All organisations and rules here are fictional.

## Opening hours

Weekdays: 09:00–17:00. Saturday: 09:00–13:00.
This policy contains no Sunday opening-hours information.

## Appointment queries

Staff confirm appointment availability in the scheduling system. Looking up a slot
does not book it. Questions that this policy cannot answer go to staff.

## Document review

Referral summaries remain drafts until a staff member checks the source document.
This policy provides no diagnosis, medication or fasting instructions.
